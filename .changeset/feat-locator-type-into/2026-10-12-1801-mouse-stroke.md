# feat(page): stroke with the mouse held down, and an element's box to aim it

- **Date:** 2026-10-12 18:01
- **Commit:** 9cc5f83
- **Area:** Page, Locator

## Problem

The mouse could click and move but never hold a button across a move. A signature drawn on a pad, or a map panned by dragging it, had no real-input path: `Locator::drag_to` goes from one element onto another, not between points. And nothing gave an element's place in the viewport in whole pixels to aim a pointer with.

## Cause

`Mouse` offered `click`, `move_to` and `wheel` only.

## Introduced

Not a regression: the API never had it.

## Fix

`Mouse::stroke` presses the left button at its first point, passes through the rest and releases at the last, as one pointer sequence. `Locator::bounding_box` returns the element's box in its frame's viewport, rounded in the page.

## Verified

The browser scenario strokes three points across a pad that records its pointer events, and requires one press first, one release last and at least two moves between. It passed on Chromium and Firefox over BiDi and over Classic. Clippy with warnings denied exited 0.
