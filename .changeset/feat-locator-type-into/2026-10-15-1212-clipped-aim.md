# fix(locator): check the hit when typing into a field, and re-scroll as Playwright does when it misses

- **Date:** 2026-10-15 12:12
- **Commit:** 4c16a73
- **Area:** Locator

## Problem

In an application's e2e, typing into a form's text field failed with `did not take focus: the click landed on null, focus is on <body>`, and the form showed its "Discard changes?" confirm. The field sat partly below the bottom edge of the scrolling form dialog, in the strip of window under it.

## Cause

`type_into` ran without the hit check, on the grounds that a label over a field forwards focus. Aimed at the centre of the field's part inside the window, it clicked a point outside the dialog, on its overlay, though the hit test named the overlay. The dialog took that as a click outside. The focus error then reported what lay at the field's full centre, off the window, hence `null`.

Making the hit required exposed a second fault. The pre-scroll nudges a target clear of whatever covers its centre, and counted any element as a cover. For a field cut off by a box that clips its overflow without a scrollbar, the element beneath the box took the hit, and each nudge reversed the last, so the field never held still.

## Introduced

5eed46d (2026-10-12), "feat(locator): type into a field like a person and check what it holds", which ran typing without the hit check. The nudge treating any element as a cover dates from e8050d3 (2026-09-12), "feat(onday): WebDriver BiDi browser automation and an MCP server".

## Fix

Typing is a pointer action, so it requires the hit as a click does, with a label bound to the field still counting as the field. While something else takes the hit, each retry first scrolls the target to the next of `end`, `center` and `start`, as Playwright's pointer actions cycle their scroll alignments. The pre-scroll nudges only for a sticky or fixed bar and leaves anything else to the hit check. The focus error reports what is at the aimed point.

## Verified

`cargo fmt --all`, `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets --all-features -- -D warnings`, `cargo test --workspace --all-features` and `cargo test -p onday --test browser -- --ignored` (6 passed). The new `#clipped` fixture, a field an `overflow: hidden` box cuts off over another element, types through the locator in Chromium and Firefox over BiDi and Classic. Before the nudge fix, the same step timed out with `still moving` in all four.
