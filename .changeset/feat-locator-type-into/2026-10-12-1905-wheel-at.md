# feat(page): turn the wheel with the pointer at a point

- **Date:** 2026-10-12 19:05
- **Commit:** d1a253a
- **Area:** Page

## Problem

A scroller inside the page, a calendar grid for one, could not be scrolled with the wheel. `Mouse::wheel` moved the page, or nothing, whatever the caller meant to scroll.

## Cause

`Mouse::wheel` passes the viewport's corner as the pointer's position, and the scroller a wheel reaches is the one under the pointer. Over Classic the fallback scrolled the window outright.

## Introduced

Not a regression: the wheel has sent from the corner since it was added.

## Fix

`Mouse::wheel_at` takes the viewport point the wheel turns at. The Classic fallback now moves the nearest scroller under that point, and the window only when there is none.

## Verified

The browser scenario turns the wheel over an 80px box holding 600px of content and requires the box to scroll while the page's own scroll position stays where it was. It passed on Chromium and Firefox over BiDi and over Classic, six tests in all. Clippy with warnings denied exited 0.
