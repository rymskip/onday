# fix(locator): name every refused attempt, and what refused a fallback drop

- **Date:** 2026-10-19 15:53
- **Commit:** 2c5ce6b
- **Area:** Locator

## Problem

onday's own scenario typing into a field a toast covers failed now and then: its error named the sticky header rather than the toast. And an application's e2e saw a drag fail with only `did not accept the drop`, which said nothing of what refused it or whether the browser had dragged at all.

## Cause

A pointer action that timed out reported its last attempt's reason alone. Since 1886433 the attempts cycle their scroll alignment as Playwright's do, so the last reason could be any of the things that took the hit in turn; Playwright's timeout carries the whole call log. A drag the browser did not finish fell back to events fired in the page, and a refused fallback drop returned a bare `none`. The native drag watch also left a pair of `once` listeners behind per drag, which an untrusted event could consume.

## Introduced

1886433 (2026-10-19), `feat(locator): take pointer actions through Playwright's retry and hit-target guard`, cycled the alignments on every retry. The bare refusal dates from e8050d3 (2026-09-12), `feat(onday): WebDriver BiDi browser automation and an MCP server`.

## Fix

A timeout names each distinct reason its attempts were refused, in order. A refused fallback drop names the element under the point that refused it and whether a native drag started and ended. The native drag watch tracks the trusted `dragstart`, `drop` and `dragend` of the current drag and removes the listeners of the one before.

## Verified

`cargo test -p onday --test browser chromium_over -- --ignored`, 2 passed over BiDi and Classic, including the covered field whose error must name the toast. `cargo fmt --all` and `cargo clippy --workspace --all-targets --all-features -- -D warnings` clean.
